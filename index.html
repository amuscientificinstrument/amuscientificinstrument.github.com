About

Built on a workshop table
in Belagavi.

AMU Scientific Instruments started as a small side table of laser-cut magnetism demonstrators, built for a handful of nearby schools that wanted their physics apparatus to actually be touched, rotated, and taken apart — not just watched from a demonstration bench.

That first batch of magnet and coil apparatus is still the core of the catalogue today. Since then, the workshop has grown to cover a wider spread of the CBSE Science and Maths syllabus, from Grade 6 elementary shapes through Grade 10 electromagnetism and trigonometry, with every instrument designed directly against the textbook chapter it supports rather than a generic "lab kit" brief.

Every design is drafted, cut, and bench-tested in-house before it's offered in the catalogue, in a choice of clear acrylic — for classrooms where seeing the internal mechanism matters — or MDF, for schools ordering at scale on a tighter budget.
